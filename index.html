01.About me

I think of myself as an ideas man. I approach building as an idea-driven problem solver—focused on creating solutions that simplify complexity and deliver real value. Goal-oriented and productivity-focused, I favor pragmatic, sometimes unconventional approaches to architecture and implementation.
